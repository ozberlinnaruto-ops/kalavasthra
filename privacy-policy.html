Section 01

Overview

Welcome to Kala Vasthras ("we", "us", or "our"). We are an Indian e-commerce and online education business operating at www.kalavasthras.in, offering authentic Indian clothing (sarees, kurtas, dress materials, dupattas) and online tailoring and fabric painting courses.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from you, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over your data.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us via WhatsApp at +91 91597 69529 or by email at namaste@kalavasthras.in.

📌 This policy applies to all visitors and customers of www.kalavasthras.in, including anyone who purchases products, enrolls in courses, or contacts us via WhatsApp or email.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways — information you provide directly to us, and information collected automatically when you use our website.

Information you provide to us:

  • Name and contact details — your full name, WhatsApp number, email address, and delivery address when you place an order or enroll in a course.
  • Payment information — payment is processed via Razorpay. We do not store your card details. Razorpay securely handles all payment data.
  • Order and course information — details of products purchased, courses enrolled in, and your communication with us regarding orders.
  • WhatsApp messages — if you contact us via WhatsApp, we retain the conversation for order support purposes.
  • Feedback and reviews — if you voluntarily submit a review or feedback, we may store and display it.
  • Appointment bookings — if you book a consultation, we collect your name, contact, preferred date and time, and garment details.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and browser data — your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited via Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
  • Cookies — small files stored on your browser to improve your experience. See Section 7 for details.
  • Referral source — how you found our website (e.g. Google search, Instagram, WhatsApp link).

Section 04

Sharing Your Data

We only share your data with trusted third parties where necessary to provide our services. We do not sell or rent your data.

  • Razorpay — our payment gateway. Your payment data is processed and stored by Razorpay under their privacy policy at razorpay.com/privacy. We do not store card numbers or CVVs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you contact us or we confirm your order via WhatsApp, messages are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. We use the standard WhatsApp Business application.
  • Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager — used to understand how visitors use our website. Google may process anonymised data as a data processor. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Courier / logistics partners — when shipping physical products, we share your name and delivery address with our shipping partners to fulfil the order.
  • Zoom — for online live classes. When you attend a Zoom session, Zoom's privacy policy applies. Your email or name may be visible to the instructor.
  • Legal authorities — if required by Indian law, court order, or government request, we may disclose your information to the appropriate authorities.

📌 All third-party services we use are reputable, India-compatible providers. We ensure they meet appropriate data protection standards.

Section 05

Payments & Razorpay

All payments on Kala Vasthras are processed through Razorpay, one of India's most trusted and RBI-compliant payment gateways. When you click "Enroll & Pay" or make a purchase, you are redirected to Razorpay's secure payment interface.

  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, or net banking credentials.
  • Razorpay encrypts all payment data using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
  • After a successful payment, Razorpay shares a transaction confirmation with us (order ID and amount only) to process your order or course enrollment.
  • For refunds, we initiate the refund via Razorpay. Refunds typically reflect in 5–7 business days to your original payment method.
🔒

Razorpay's full privacy policy is available at razorpay.com/privacy. For payment disputes, please contact us on WhatsApp within 7 days of purchase.

WhatsApp Communications

We use WhatsApp as our primary customer support and order communication channel. When you contact us via WhatsApp at +91 91597 69529, please note the following:

  • Your WhatsApp messages and phone number are stored in our WhatsApp Business account for customer service purposes.
  • We may send you order confirmations, shipping updates, course details, and appointment confirmations via WhatsApp.
  • If you have purchased from us before, we may occasionally message you about new collections or offers. You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any message.
  • We do not share your WhatsApp number with any third party for marketing purposes.
  • WhatsApp messages between you and us are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
Section 07

Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files placed on your device — to improve performance and understand visitor behaviour. We use the following types of cookies:

Cookie Type Purpose Can You Opt Out?
Essential cookies Required for the website to function (e.g. shopping cart, session management) No — necessary for service
Analytics cookies Google Analytics — tracks page visits, session duration, and traffic sources (anonymised) Yes — via browser settings
Marketing cookies Google Tag Manager — tracks conversions from ads or external links Yes — via browser settings

You can disable or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website. To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Section 08

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by Indian law.

  • Order and payment records — retained for 7 years as required under Indian tax and accounting laws.
  • Course enrollment records — retained for the duration of your access and up to 3 years thereafter.
  • WhatsApp conversations — retained for up to 2 years for customer service reference.
  • Analytics data — Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default (as set by Google).
  • Marketing consent records — retained until you withdraw consent, plus 1 year.

After the applicable retention period, your personal data is securely deleted or anonymised. You may request earlier deletion — see Section 9 for your rights.

Section 09

Your Rights

As a user of Kala Vasthras, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via WhatsApp or email.

  • Right to access — you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — if any information we hold about you is inaccurate, you can ask us to correct it.
  • Right to deletion — you can request that we delete your personal data, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records.
  • Right to withdraw consent — if you have opted in to marketing messages, you can withdraw consent at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or emailing us.
  • Right to object — you can object to our processing of your data for direct marketing or where we rely on legitimate interest.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format where technically feasible.

📌 We will respond to all data rights requests within 30 days. To submit a request, WhatsApp us at +91 91597 69529 or email namaste@kalavasthras.in with the subject line "Data Rights Request".

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email and we will take steps to delete the information.

For users aged 13–18, we recommend parental guidance when making purchases or enrolling in courses online.

Section 11

Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

  • Our website uses HTTPS encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect data transmitted between your browser and our server.
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS certified.
  • Access to customer data is restricted to authorised team members only.
  • We do not store full payment card details on our systems.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

⚠️

If you receive any suspicious message claiming to be from Kala Vasthras asking for payment details, OTPs, or passwords — please do not respond and report it to us immediately on WhatsApp.
